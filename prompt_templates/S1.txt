Select memories whose joint analysis is likely to produce useful, evidence-grounded knowledge.

INPUT
Root question, research-tree context, eligible memory catalogue, S2 token budget and previous-attempt exclusions.

Evaluate synthesizability using five dimensions, scored 0–5:

R: Relevance to the research questions.
C: Complementary evidence, methods or explanations.
X: Meaningful contradictions worth examining.
N: Potential for new understanding beyond summarisation.
E: Adequacy of evidence for substantive analysis.

Total = R + C + X + N + E.

Memories addressing the same question receive a relevance advantage, but selection may cross research branches.

Prefer focused, coherent combinations with a specific analytical objective. Do not include weakly related memories merely because context space is available.

Select at least two eligible memories. Exclude previously attempted combinations and their subsets.
Both research and synthesis memories in the catalogue are eligible. An existing
synthesis may contribute its knowledge units to further analysis; raw evidence is
not required for every input. Assess every listed memory without inventing omissions.
