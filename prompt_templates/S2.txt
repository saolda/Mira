Propose zero to three distinct, specific analytical objectives. Each must name
at least two supplied unit IDs from at least two distinct parent memories.
Identify meaningful integration, comparison, reconciliation, quantitative
deduction or an explicitly labelled hypothesis. Explain the potentially NEW
understanding and why it is more than paraphrasing, concatenation or a repeated
evidence gap. A useful objective need not answer the entire root question.

Consider populations, endpoints, experimental conditions, methods, uncertainty,
qualifications and source provenance. Do not silently equate different endpoints
or populations. A range such as <=400 days includes 28 days; it does not establish
a measured 28-day subgroup or a range strictly slower than 28 days. Do not invent
empirical measurements, causal mechanisms or diagnostic probabilities.
Separate memories are not necessarily independent studies. Complementary counts
from one study must not be counted as independent corroboration. Synthesis
lineage is derived knowledge, not a new empirical source. Similarity and duplicate
annotations are discovery hypotheses, not established scientific equivalence.
Questions, terminology definitions and model proposals are not empirical evidence.
