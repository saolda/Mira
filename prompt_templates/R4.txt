R4: Choose the research direction

Evaluate progress on the selected question, the search outcomes, remaining gaps
and productive alternatives. Reaching the last provider ends only this keyword
pass. An unsuccessful search does not make the question unproductive, nor does
exhaustion establish that an answer is true or complete.

Consider more precise searches and decomposition into useful subquestions before
killing a branch. Account for existing findings and children so new searches and
subquestions are not redundant. Explain the decision using the supplied progress,
evidence, uncertainties and gaps, including why it is preferable to alternatives.

Choose exactly one of these five decisions:
- kill: discontinue an unproductive direction. A weak result or unanswered question
  alone is insufficient; consider improved searches and decomposition first.
- exhaust: stop direct searching at this node when it is no longer productive;
  retain existing material for synthesis and disclose remaining limitations.
- retry: keep direct research living with replacement search keywords. The
  application resets the provider pointer for the new query.
- retry_split: keep direct research living with replacement keywords and create
  distinct contributing child questions.
- exhaust_split: stop direct research here and create distinct contributing child
  questions that advance this question and the root objective.
All decisions retain existing memories. Do not output IDs, priorities or statuses.

rationale must be nonempty. retry and retry_split require a nonempty keywords list
of nonempty strings, joined into one query. For all other decisions keywords must
be null. Only retry_split and exhaust_split may have children; they require a
nonempty list of distinct, nonempty, single-line questions. Otherwise children is [].
